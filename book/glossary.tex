\chapter*{Glossary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Glossary}

This glossary collects the words that travel most easily between statistical physics, graphical
modeling, neural computation, and experimental biology.  Each entry lists the meanings the word
carries in those literatures and states what this book requires before the word is used in a
claim.  The entries are grouped by the space in which the word is most often anchored; within each
group they are ordered alphabetically.  The notation chapter fixes symbols; this chapter fixes
usage.

Two habits make this glossary redundant.  Before using any term above, name the space in which
the object lives and the alternative explanations that have been excluded; after using it, keep
the carried object visible until the validation endpoint has been declared.  The book's chapters
repeat those two requirements in progressively stronger forms.

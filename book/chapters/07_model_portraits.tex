For neural models, the distinction needed throughout the chapter is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\alpha_{ij}^{(h)}(x)&:\quad \text{where one layer reads},\\
\Phi_{t:s}(x)&:\quad \text{how stored information crosses later transitions},\\
G_{i\leftarrow j}(C)&:\quad \text{how the complete categorical prediction changes}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The first two are internal computational objects.  The last is the externally comparable
response.  The following sections derive the correspondences and specify the assumptions under
which their native probability laws or update rules can also be matched.

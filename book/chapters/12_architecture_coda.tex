\section*{Coda: capacity and execution require separate ledgers}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Coda: capacity and execution require separate ledgers}

This chapter moved from routing geometry to persistent pair state, reusable operator dictionaries,
ordered composition, sparse execution, and structural alignment.  These constructions answer how
a relational computation can be represented and executed.  Their algebraic capacity, stored
parameters, arithmetic work, memory traffic, training stability, and biological performance remain
separate coordinates.

The next chapter imposes a different kind of constraint.  A claimed conditional interaction must
remain measurable when its endpoint identities are varied.  That requirement restricts which
information a router may use and creates exact recoverability limits.  Architecture design then
becomes an information problem as well as a capacity and systems problem.

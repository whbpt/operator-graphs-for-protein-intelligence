Linear double projection preserves marginals to first order.  Multiplicative matrix scaling
factors through the same quotient and enforces those constraints at finite displacement.  It
also has the exact variational form
\begin{equation}
Q^*=\underset{Q\in\mathcal U(p_i,p_j)}{\operatorname{argmin}}
\operatorname{KL}(Q\|\overline K),
\qquad
\overline K=\frac{\exp(S)}{\sum_{a,b}\exp S(a,b)}.
\label{eq:book-sinkhorn-information-projection}
\end{equation}
For $S=-E/\tau$, the objective is an energy--entropy competition.  A physical equilibrium reading
requires the corresponding energy, temperature, and ensemble; the information projection is
exact without that reading.  Even exact pair laws need not assemble into one joint law, as the
three-pair counterexample shows.

The next part applies these coordinates to familiar models, distinguishing their native
probability laws, estimators, and update rules before comparing their edge operators.

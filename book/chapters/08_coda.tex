\section*{Coda: the estimand survives the pipeline only by declaration}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Coda: the estimand survives the pipeline only by declaration}

Weighting sequences, fitting a model, centering tables, averaging responses, scalarizing blocks,
and subtracting nulls generally do not commute.  The estimand fixes which pipeline is relevant.
The four-term ledger then separates family-sampling error, model misspecification, pLM transfer,
and response-sampling error as far as the available observations permit.

A Potts block describes a fitted family law; a pLM block describes a predictor's response to
substitution.  Their agreement can establish a shared categorical pattern.  Biological
interpretation requires the endpoint-specific tests developed in
Chapter~\ref{chap:book-falsification}.

The next chapter follows the evidence through the three AlphaFold generations.  Spectral
analysis comes afterward, once the graph and its retained information have been declared.

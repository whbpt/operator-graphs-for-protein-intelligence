This chapter compares the native states, edge objects, and laws of familiar models before
extracting their common operator coordinates.  A static Potts block, a Gaussian precision block,
a marginalized RBM component, and a Transformer route enter through different constructions.
The question is which correspondence survives a declared extraction.

The chapter has four blocks: probabilistic models; neural routes, state transport, and categorical
response; the 2019 reconstruction framework; and the graph-network lineage.  The two historical
cases show what exact correspondences made computationally transferable and which differences
remained afterward.  Throughout, the audit names the state space, normalized variable, edge
carrier, estimator, and first claim that needs additional evidence.

\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Coda: compression is a scientific choice}

Support thresholding selects edges; low-rank approximation selects categorical directions; pair
truncation selects interaction orders; scalarization selects one summary per edge.  Agreement
after one reduction can conceal disagreement before it, as the same-shadow example demonstrates.

An ANOVA component is irreducible to lower orders under its declared variables and reference.
Its origin may be a many-body energy, a marginalized latent variable, or nonlinear computation.
The decomposition alone does not choose among them.

These constructions have used linear projection.  A finite pair probability law must also remain
positive and obey its marginal constraints.  The next chapter connects the linear gauge to this
nonlinear probability geometry.

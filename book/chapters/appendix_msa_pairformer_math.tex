\section{The MSA Pairformer mathematical interlude}
\label{sec:book-msa-pairformer-math}

This section collects derivations that support the main Pairformer case but are optional for its
architectural and estimand-level argument.  The preceding case uses the finite cross-moment, the query
conditioned row measure, the triangle path, and the categorical response as its primary objects.

\subsection{Measure derivatives, ranks, and typed maps}

Writing $\phi_s(q)=r_s(q;M)$ and
$Y_s^{ij}=\chi_{si}\chi_{sj}(u_{si}\otimes v_{sj})$, the complete-mask derivative is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal O_{q,ij}=\mathbb E_{\mu_q}Y_s^{ij},\qquad
\nabla_q\mathcal O_{q,ij}
=\operatorname{Cov}_{\mu_q}\!\left(Y_s^{ij},\nabla_q\phi_s(q)\right),
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-query-covariance-gradient}
\end{equation}
when the feature map has no direct query argument.  Gaps or query-dependent features add feature
and denominator terms.  Higher derivatives contain higher cumulants of the tilted empirical law.

For one feature channel, let $U^a_{is}=u^a_{si}$, $V^b_{js}=v^b_{sj}$, and
$D=\operatorname{diag}(\omega)$.  Before nonlinear mixing,
\begin{equation}
\mathcal O_{ij}^{ab}=U^a_{i:}DV^{b\mathsf T}_{:j},
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}(U^aDV^{b\mathsf T})\leq\min(S,L).
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-cross-moment-rank}
\end{equation}
For a linearly combined output channel,
\begin{equation}
\mathcal O^d=\sum_{s=1}^{S}\omega_sA_sW_dB_s^{\mathsf T},
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}(\mathcal O^d)\leq\min(L,Sc).
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-output-channel-rank}
\end{equation}
Masks make coefficients pair dependent, and gates, residual depth, and triangle nonlinearities remove
any global low-rank conclusion about the final contact map.

The three typed maps can be written as
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf K_{q,\theta}:&\quad M\longmapsto
\left[\int A_\theta(m_i)\otimes B_\theta(m_j)\,d\widehat\mu_{q,ij}[M](m)\right]_{ij},\\
\mathsf P_{\theta,Z}:&\quad M\longmapsto
\left[\sum_jP_{Z,\theta}(i,j)V_\theta(m_{sj})\right]_{si},\\
\mathsf G_\theta:&\quad Z\longmapsto
\left[\gamma_L\sum_ka_\theta(Z_{ik})\odot b_\theta(Z_{jk})\right]_{ij}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-typed-operators}
\end{equation}
The finite residual block is their ordered nonlinear composition.  A continuum path limit requires a
declared scaling such as $\gamma_L\asymp L^{-1}$; it is not implied by the finite implementation.

If rows were independent draws from $P$ and features were bounded, the cross-moment has the formal
limit
\begin{equation}
\widehat{\mathcal O}_{q,ij}
\xrightarrow[S\to\infty]{\mathsf P}
\frac{\mathbb E_{M\sim P}[\chi_i\chi_j e^{\phi_\theta(q,M)}
A_\theta(M_i)\otimes B_\theta(M_j)]}
{\mathbb E_{M\sim P}[\chi_i\chi_j e^{\phi_\theta(q,M)}]}.
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-mean-field-opm}
\end{equation}
With $\gamma_L\sim L^{-1}$, the triangle sum has the graphon-style formal limit
\begin{equation}
(\mathsf G_{\mathrm{out}}Z)(x,y)
=\int_0^1a_Z(x,u)\odot b_Z(y,u)\,d\nu(u).
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-graphon-triangle-limit}
\end{equation}

The linear OPM write has a parameterization gauge.  For invertible $R,T\in GL(c)$,
$A\mapsto RA$, $B\mapsto TB$, and
$W_{\rm opm}\mapsto W_{\rm opm}(R\otimes T)^{-1}$ preserve the write up to vectorisation.
The masked loss therefore identifies an output law up to output-equivalent parameters, not a unique
factorization into row weights and feature maps.

\subsection{Router curvature and effective depth}

Before LayerNorm, the two query--key fields combine into the bilinear kernel
\begin{equation}
h_{1,si}-\lambda h_{2,si}
=x_{0i}^{\mathsf T}
\underbrace{(W_{Q1}^{\mathsf T}W_{K1}-\lambda W_{Q2}^{\mathsf T}W_{K2})}_{B_\lambda}x_{si},
\qquad \operatorname{rank}(B_\lambda)\leq2d_q.
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-effective-bilinear-kernel}
\end{equation}
For a finite score vector $r$,
\begin{equation}
\log\sum_se^{r_s}
=\sup_{\omega\in\Delta_S}\left\{\sum_s\omega_sr_s+H(\omega)\right\},
\qquad H(\omega)=-\sum_s\omega_s\log\omega_s,
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-entropy-variational}
\end{equation}
with optimizer $\omega=\operatorname{softmax}(r)$.  Its curvature is
\begin{equation}
\nabla_r^2\log\sum_se^{r_s}
=\operatorname{diag}(\omega)-\omega\omega^{\mathsf T}.
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-softmax-curvature}
\end{equation}

The differential of the cross-moment separates feature and router terms:
\begin{equation}
d\mathcal O_{q,ij}
=\mathbb E_{\mu_{q,ij}}[d(u_i\otimes v_j)]
+\operatorname{Cov}_{\mu_{q,ij}}(u_i\otimes v_j,dr)
+d_{q,ij}^{-1}\,\text{mask/denominator terms}.
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-total-moment-differential}
\end{equation}
The corresponding pair-specific effective depth is
\begin{equation}
N_{\mathrm{eff}}^{ij}
=\frac{d_{q,ij}^{2}}{\sum_s(\omega_s\chi_{si}\chi_{sj})^2}.
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-effective-depth}
\end{equation}
At depth, row dependence requires the covariance form
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{Var}(\mathcal O_{ij})
=\sum_{s,t}\alpha_{s|ij}\alpha_{t|ij}\operatorname{Cov}(Y_s^{ij},Y_t^{ij}),
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-dependent-row-variance}
\end{equation}
with a declared tree or resampling scheme.

\subsection{Symmetry, residual transport, and readouts}

For a fixed query row and a permutation $\pi$ of the remaining rows,
\begin{equation}
M\mapsto\pi M,\qquad \omega(\pi M)=\pi\omega(M),\qquad
\mathcal O_q(\pi M)=\mathcal O_q(M).
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-row-equivariance}
\end{equation}
Changing the query is an intervention because it changes the reference law.  Independent permutations
of two chains preserve their marginals while generally changing their paired joint table.

Collect the MSA and pair states into $x_\ell=(M_\ell,Z_\ell)$.  The residual transport is
\begin{equation}
x_{\ell+1}=x_\ell+F_\ell(x_\ell),
\qquad
D x_D=\left[\prod_{\ell=D-1}^{0}(I+DF_\ell(x_\ell))\right]D x_0.
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-residual-transport}
\end{equation}
The local Jacobian has the two-track form
\begin{equation}
J_\ell=
\begin{pmatrix}I+J_{MM}^{(\ell)}&J_{MZ}^{(\ell)}\\
J_{ZM}^{(\ell)}&I+J_{ZZ}^{(\ell)}\end{pmatrix}.
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-two-track-jacobian}
\end{equation}
Singular values of the ordered product diagnose amplification and forgetting; residual form alone
does not give a Lyapunov or free-energy interpretation.

The independent scalar readouts from the pair state are
\begin{equation}
\widehat c^{(r)}_{ij}
=\frac12\left[\sigma(\eta_r^{\mathsf T}\operatorname{LN}(Z^{(\ell_r)}_{ij})+b_r)
+\sigma(\eta_r^{\mathsf T}\operatorname{LN}(Z^{(\ell_r)}_{ji})+b_r)\right],
\quad (\ell_r,r)\in\{(15,\mathrm C_\beta),(18,\mathrm{ConFind})\}.
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-independent-readouts}
\end{equation}
The hidden directed pair field, scalar contact probability, and categorical response remain distinct
objects.

\subsection{Pair assignment and response audits}

If a fraction $\rho$ of rows is correctly paired and the wrong pairs are conditionally independent,
the centered cross-chain moment obeys the attenuation approximation
\begin{equation}
\mathbb E\widehat C_{ij}^{\Pi}=\rho C_{ij}^{\star}+(1-\rho)C_{ij}^{\mathrm{wrong}}
\approx\rho C_{ij}^{\star}.
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-pairing-attenuation}
\end{equation}
Sampling and assignment uncertainty separate as
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{Var}_{X,\Pi}(\widehat C)
=\mathbb E_\Pi\operatorname{Var}_X(\widehat C\mid\Pi)
+\operatorname{Var}_\Pi\mathbb E_X(\widehat C\mid\Pi).
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-pairing-total-variance}
\end{equation}

For one organism with $n$ candidates, the idealized assignment is
\begin{equation}
P_g^\star\in\arg\max_{P\in\mathcal P_n}\langle P,C_g(W)\rangle.
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-hungarian-assignment}
\end{equation}
Gumbel--Sinkhorn relaxation and EM-like hard reassignment solve moving, noisy objectives; monotonicity
of exact coordinate ascent is therefore not guaranteed for the released procedure.

For a source perturbation $\delta M$, the difference between adaptive and frozen routing is
\begin{equation}
D\mathcal O_{ij}^{\mathrm{adaptive}}[\delta M]-D\mathcal O_{ij}^{\mathrm{frozen}}[\delta M]
=\sum_sD\omega_s[\delta M]\frac{\chi_{si}\chi_{sj}}{d_{q,ij}}
\left(u_{si}\otimes v_{sj}-\mathcal O_{q,ij}\right).
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-router-mediated-response}
\end{equation}
A static pairwise Hamiltonian requires the additional reciprocity test
\begin{equation}
G_{i\leftarrow j}^{\mathcal M}=G_{j\leftarrow i}^{\mathcal M\mathsf T}.
\label{eq:msa-pairformer-reciprocity-test}
\end{equation}
under a common endpoint-excluding context.  Query-only training and scalar symmetrization do not
impose this condition on the hidden response.
